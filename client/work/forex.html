One controlled workflow for every forex request, from capture to settlement. A shared, server-based system that any bank can run: commercial banks with branch networks, and central banks overseeing exchange control. It takes each application through a four-eyes workflow, keeps clients and staff informed automatically, and records every step in a complete audit trail.

The problem

Forex requests pass through too many hands to track

In any bank, a foreign currency application moves from the branch inputter to an authoriser, Treasury and Exchange Control before it's funded and closed. Every hand-off is a chance for a document to go missing, a deadline to pass or a client to be left waiting without news.

This system gives everyone one place to work. An application captured at a branch appears for the authoriser, Treasury and Exchange Control straight away, and documents uploaded at one PC open at any other, because they all live on the same central database.

It fits the whole sector. Commercial banks run it across their branches, from capture to payment. Central banks can use the same workflow, roles and audit trail for exchange control oversight, reviewing and approving requests with every decision on record. A white-label edition takes each institution's own name, logo and colours.

How an application flows

Six steps, each with its own owner

  1. Capture · Inputter
  2. Authorise · Authoriser
  3. Allocate · Treasury
  4. Fund · Auto or client
  5. Process · Exchange Control
  6. Close · Reconcile

Handled automatically along the way: returns and rejections, the 16:00 funding cut-off and forfeiture, part payments, closing at the client's request, payment reversal, and blocking clients with unreconciled advance payments.

What it does

Built for the way a bank actually works

Four-eyes controls

Maker-checker, own-branch rules, invoice and per-diem limits, and access rights by role or user.

Smart allocation

Any currency with rate and cross rate; a funds check against the core-banking balance skips the wait when money is there.

Automatic communication

Every client contact emailed or texted at each step, with scheduled reminders and branch alerts that are never sent twice.

Reconciliation built in

Advance payments tracked for 90 days with documents, authorisation and Exchange Control sign-off.

Reports & audit

Work queues, period reports and a full per-record audit, exported to Excel, Word, PDF, CSV, HTML or print.

Operations ready

End-of-day, month and year routines, encrypted backups, and clearly badged UAT and LIVE environments.

Try it yourself

A working demo in your browser

The demo is the Prototype edition, set up for a fictional "Demo Bank". It runs entirely in your browser with sample data, so nothing you enter is sent anywhere and you can't break anything.

On the sign-in page, open Prototype demo accounts and sign in as any role with the shared password Demo@2026. For example, raise an application as an Inputter, then sign in as an Authoriser, Treasury Officer or Exchange Control to move it along.
