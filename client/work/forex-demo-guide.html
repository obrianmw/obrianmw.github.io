Demo user manual

How to use the Forex demo

The demo is the Prototype edition of the Forex Applications System, set up for a fictional "Demo Bank". Follow one application from the branch to payment by signing in as each role in turn.

Open the demo ↗

1 · Before you start

Safe to explore

Everything runs in your own browser. The customers and accounts are simulated, nothing you type is sent to a server, and no real emails or text messages go out. Messages the system would send appear under Outgoing messages instead.

Your changes stay in this browser until you reset them. To start again, sign in as an administrator and choose Reset to sample data on the Demo data page.

The demo has its own clock. Use the System date controls in the top bar (+1h, +1d, +7d) to move time forward and watch deadlines, reminders and the 16:00 funding cut-off take effect.

3 · Walkthrough

Take one application from start to finish

  1. Capture it Inputter · Grace Banda

    Click + New application. Enter an account number, or a customer number and Find the customer's accounts, then pick the account. Name, account type and balance come from the simulated core banking system.

    Choose the transaction type, amount and currency, fill in the beneficiary and upload the documents the type needs (any PDF works in the demo). Then Submit to authoriser, or Save as draft to finish later.

  2. Authorise it Authoriser · James Mwale

    Sign out, sign in as James and open Pending authorisation. Review the application and add a comment, then Authorise it, return it to the inputter to correct, or Reject & advise client. An authoriser can never approve an application they captured themselves.

  3. Allocate the currency Treasury · Thandiwe Nkhoma

    Open Awaiting allocation and click Allocate forex. Allocate in full or in part, with the rate (MWK per unit) and a cross rate if the currency differs.

    If the client's account already holds enough money, it is debited straight away. Otherwise the client must fund it by 16:00, and the branch or Exchange Control marks it funded once the money is in. Move the system date past 16:00 to see an unfunded allocation forfeited.

  4. Pay it Exchange Control · Mphatso Gondwe

    Open the funded application and record the payment as Fully paid or Partly paid. The client, inputter and branch are notified, and a full payment closes the record. A payment can also be reversed with a reason, which reopens it.

  5. Reconcile advance payments Inputter, Authoriser, Exchange Control

    Advance payments for imports must be reconciled within 90 days. The inputter uploads the customs entry and final invoice, the authoriser approves, and Exchange Control marks it reconciled. Until then, new applications from that client are blocked.

  6. Look around Everyone

    Every role has a dashboard; click any chart or number to see the applications behind it. Reports cover payments, processed and all transactions, demand, client history, reconciliation and the audit trail, and export to Excel, Word, PDF, CSV or print. Open the ✉ inbox to see the emails and texts the system would have sent.

4 · Administrators

Running the system

Sign in as it.admin@demobank.example to manage users and access rights, branches, message templates, rules and rates (travel allowance, funding cut-off, reconciliation days), reference number formats and branding: the bank's name, logo and colours.

With two administrators, adding or changing a user creates a request that the other administrator must approve. Try it: make a change as the System Administrator, then sign in as the Backup Administrator to approve it from the dashboard.
