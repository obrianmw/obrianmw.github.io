Customer Notification System

Built for a bank in Malawi. Core banking tells the system about credits, debits and account events, and the system emails the customer. Staff also use it for monthly statements, service messages and marketing campaigns to mailing lists.

How it fits together

Alerts in, emails out

Core banking posts each transaction and account event to the system's API. Each one becomes an alert email in a queue, and a background worker delivers it with retries, duplicate protection and priority, so security alerts always go first.

Staff work in the web app alongside it: they send statements and service messages, and run marketing campaigns to mailing lists. Contacts who have unsubscribed are always skipped, and unsubscribing never stops a customer's account alerts.

What it does

Everything a customer should hear about

Transaction alerts

Deposits, transfers, card purchases, withdrawals, failed transactions, low balances and unusual-activity security alerts.

Account events

Account opened or closed, card blocked or unblocked, and loan repayments due or overdue.

Statements & notices

Monthly statements built from reported transactions, plus staff-written notices sent once or on a schedule.

Mailing lists & campaigns

Lists with CSV import and export, scheduled campaigns, and one-click unsubscribe on every marketing email.

Reports & exports

Filter by date, branch, type, channel or status and download to Excel, with every export audit-logged.

Security & branding

Argon2 passwords, lockout, CSRF protection, masked account numbers, and a branding panel for logo, colours and email look.

Try it yourself

A live demo with sample data

The demo runs for a fictional "Demo Bank" with about 150 sample customers and their transactions. Emails are only written to Sent emails, never actually sent, and the demo resets to its sample data whenever it restarts. It's on a free server that sleeps when nobody is using it, so the first visit can take about a minute to load.

Every account uses the password Demo@2026. The sign-ins are also listed on the demo's login page.
