IM Collections & IM Closet

Logos and a brand identity for a fashion, home and lifestyle brand and its sub-brand. The lead concept, "The Hanger", has a rose-gold clothes hanger holding the initials, with a flowing script name.

The idea

Elegant, warm and easy to recognise

One mark had to work for two names. The hanger frames the "IM" initials in Bodoni, and the script wordmark changes from Collections to Closet, so both brands read as one family. "Fashion · Home · Lifestyle" sits beneath in light, spaced Montserrat.

The kit covers primary, horizontal and icon versions in colour, black, white and dark, a profile icon for social media, and print files for gift bags, tags and packaging tape. I also explored two further directions, "The Arch" and "The Bow", shown at the end of this page.
