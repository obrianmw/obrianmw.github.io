School Management System

One system for running a school, or several. Records, fees and online payments, payroll, academics, notifications and daily operations, with a portal for parents, students, teachers and the bursar, and a back office for school staff.

The idea

Bank-grade controls for a school

Schools handle money, children's records and staff pay, so the system borrows the controls I use in banking: maker-checker authorisation for voids, refunds and new accounts, a double-entry ledger with a trial balance, access rights by role and user, account lockout, a full audit trail, and end-of-day routines.

Several schools share one installation but stay completely separate. A system administrator looks after the whole platform, and each school's administrators run their own school, with its own currency, branding and message templates.

What it does

Everything a school office runs on

Fees & online payments

Fee items per term and grade, invoices, receipts, statements and late fees, with card and mobile money payments through Flutterwave, Stripe, M-Pesa and MTN.

Academics

Timetables with clash checks, attendance registers, a mark sheet that pastes from Excel, grading and report cards with class position.

Payroll & payouts

Salary profiles, tax bands and payslips. HR prepares, a second person approves, and salaries are paid by transfer, mobile money or by hand.

Notifications

Receipts, invoices, absence alerts, results and fee reminders by SMS, email or in-app, sent by a jobs runner and retried on failure.

Operations

Library loans and fines, transport routes, boarding rooms, confidential clinic visits and stores that never go below zero.

Security & audit

Two-step sign-in, account lockout, computer blocking, work queues, reports and an audit log of every change: who, what, when and from where.

Try it yourself

A live demo school

The demo runs a sample school, "Demo Academy", as a test system: messages are never sent to real phones or mailboxes, and only sandbox payments work. Actions that would break the demo for others, such as changing the shared passwords or declaring go-live, are switched off, and the demo resets whenever it restarts. It's on a free server that sleeps when unused, so the first visit can take about a minute to load.

Every account uses the password demo-password-123. On the sign-in page, open Demo accounts and click one to fill it in.
