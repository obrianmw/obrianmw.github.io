01 / About

A banker with a programmer's mindset

I've worked at Centenary Bank Ltd, Mzuzu Branch since 2019, starting when it was still New Finance Bank and MyBucks Banking Corporation. My work covers customer service, KYC and onboarding, teller operations, and the daily reconciliations that keep the branch's books balanced.

I've kept a 100% accuracy record on data entry and branch income and expense reconciliations for four years, with zero financial loss. Colleagues come to me when systems get in the way, which is how I came to be recognised as one of the three most prominent employees in the region for technical support.

In December 2025 I completed a Diploma in ICT at Mzuzu University. I combine programming, databases and advanced Excel to automate reporting and improve efficiency, and I bring the same care to brand identity and graphic design through Obrian Graphics.
